This page summarizes ways we provide user support for questions/help regarding Lead DBS and Lead Connectome:

**[Slack User Channel](https://leadsuite.slack.com/) – [Autoinvite here](https://communityinviter.com/apps/leadsuite/user-channel).**

[The Slack channel](https://leadsuite.slack.com/) ([autoinvite yourself here](https://communityinviter.com/apps/leadsuite/user-channel)) provides fast and uncomplicated help from users to users. Also, most of us are online often and usually respond fast to questions. If you are not familiar with Slack, it is a great way to communicate on research topics and you can imagine it very similar to WhatsApp or other chat systems. There are [great apps for Slack](https://slack.com/downloads) which allow you to easily communicate with us from your Desktop or mobile device.

**Knowledgebase**

You may find the walkthrough video for BIDS transition and mapping between old and new filenames here.

Other good sources of info are the [walkthrough videos section](/helpsupport/knowledge-base/walkthrough-videos/) and the [Lead-DBS manual](http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=71). If you just wish to get a quick overview about what Lead-DBS does, there is also a [screenshot section](/helpsupport/knowledge-base/screenshots/).
