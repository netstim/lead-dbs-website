We’re excited that a visualization made with Lead-DBS from [the connectomic DBS study spearheaded by Carlos Baldermann in Biological Psychiatry](https://www.biologicalpsychiatryjournal.com/article/S0006-3223(19)30003-4/abstract) was featured on the coverart of the journal. Carlos used tractography in Lead-DBS to estimate and validate fibertracts that are associated with beneficial treatment effects of deep brain stimulation to the anterior limb of the internal capsule for treatment of Obsessive Compulsive Disorder. The full study [can be found here](https://www.biologicalpsychiatryjournal.com/article/S0006-3223(19)30003-4/abstract) or on our [publications page](/about/publications/). See [this page for a gallery with all coverarts](/about/publications/) we know of that were made with Lead-DBS.
