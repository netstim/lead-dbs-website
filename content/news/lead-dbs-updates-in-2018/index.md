Three months into 2018, there have been several studies that used Lead-DBS to answer very interesting questions briefly summarized below.1. [Siobhan Ewert](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Siobhan_Ewert) and Todd Herrington could recently show that automated segmentations of the STN and GPi performed with Lead-DBS are nearly as precise as manual expert segmentations of the same structures in both a high-definition MRI sample as well as a “typical clinical” quality sample. In total, they ran >11.000 nonlinear brain deformations and segmented 120 brains manually to evaluate i) which deformation algorithm & preset yields best results and ii) how much error is still obtained when using the best strategy.
