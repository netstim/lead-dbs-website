If you have problems with PaCER or TRAC/CORE, it is also possible to pre-reconstruct using the manual reconstruction option. For this, select **Pre-reconstruct** from the **Reconstruction** pane, choose **Manual** as the method and select **Run**.

![Manual reconstruction — screenshot](manual/abb07baa28c2bd2f.png)

![Manual reconstruction — screenshot](manual/6044de8136d4d377.png)

![Manual reconstruction — screenshot](manual/1934d97f433bd571.png)

1. Two SPM windows will pop up, one with the images and one with the next steps. If you want, you can adjust the contrast in this stage.
2. Start with selecting the right hand side electrode first. Be careful, this is not the right side of the patient, but **your right side**. Move the cursor around to place the intersection of the two blue sides to the tip of the electrode in all axes. It is possible to zoom in and out using the toolbar. When you're happy with your selection, select **OK** in the other window.

   ![Manual reconstruction — screenshot](manual/b4e0f01bf9ef4ea6.png)

   ![Manual reconstruction — screenshot](manual/9a0a5e619bac62e9.png)

3. Then select a point in the trajectory, again of the electrode **to your right side**. When you're happy with your selection, select **OK**.

   ![Manual reconstruction — screenshot](manual/132cdb63ae5f202c.png)

4. Repeat with the other electrode and you're done!
5. Continue with **Localize DBS electrodes** to fine tune the electrode localization, as explained in [Electrode reconstruction](/manual/lead-dbs/step5-optional-reconstruction-of-electrode-trajectories/).
