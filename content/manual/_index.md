## About Lead-DBS

Lead-DBS is an open-source MATLAB toolbox for DBS imaging. It was first developed at the [Movement Disorders Unit of Andrea Kühn](https://neurologie.charite.de/forschung/arbeitsgruppen/bewegungsstoerungen_andrea_kuehn), Department of Neurology, Charité – University Medicine, Berlin. Since 2019, the [Network Stimulation Laboratory of Andreas Horn](https://www.netstim.org) has coordinated development.

Ask questions in the [user Slack](/helpsupport/slack-user-channel/), practise with [Lead Tutor](/helpsupport/learn-lead-dbs-yourself/) or see [how to contribute](/manual/ways-to-contribute-to-lead-dbs/). The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/netstim/leaddbs); news is on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/lead-dbs.org).

## Dependencies

Lead-DBS builds on open-source neuroimaging tools. **Cite the tools your analysis uses** as well as Lead-DBS: see [software dependencies and references](/about/lead-dbs-dependencies/).
