## Key features

- Linear and nonlinear registration of MRI and CT images
- Electrode trajectory reconstruction and manual localization correction
- Visualization in 2D slice views and 3D models of electrodes and target areas
- Calculation of the Volume of Activated Tissue (VAT)
- Structural connectivity analysis from the VAT to other brain regions
- Group analysis with Lead Group

## About Lead-DBS

Lead-DBS is an open-source, MATLAB-based toolbox for localizing and visualizing deep brain stimulation electrodes. This guide covers single-patient workflows, group analyses, connectomics and scripting.

Lead-DBS was initially developed at the [Movement Disorders Unit of Andrea Kühn](https://neurologie.charite.de/forschung/arbeitsgruppen/bewegungsstoerungen_andrea_kuehn), Department of Neurology, Charité – University Medicine, Berlin. Since 2019, the [Network Stimulation Laboratory of Andreas Horn](https://www.netstim.org) has coordinated development.

For troubleshooting and questions, join the [Slack user channel](/helpsupport/slack-user-channel/). New users can also explore the [Lead-Tutor learning resources](/helpsupport/learn-lead-dbs-yourself/). To help improve the project, see [how to contribute](/manual/ways-to-contribute-to-lead-dbs/).

Find the project on [GitHub](https://github.com/netstim/leaddbs) and [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/lead-dbs.org).

## Dependencies

Lead-DBS integrates open-source neuroimaging tools alongside code developed by the team. When using Lead-DBS for research, **please cite the underlying tools and dependencies that your analysis uses**, as well as Lead-DBS itself. See the [software dependencies and references](/about/lead-dbs-dependencies/).
