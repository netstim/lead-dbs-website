Lead-OR is a sister application to Lead-DBS, built as an extension for [3D Slicer](https://www.slicer.org/) (part of [SlicerNetstim](https://github.com/netstim/SlicerNetstim)). It is meant to assist research in the operating theatre when carrying out DBS surgeries. It directly builds upon Lead-DBS based coregistrations and segmentations and offers a real-time application to visualize invasive recordings (local field potentials and microelectrode recordings), as well as to model stimulation impact onto fiber tracts.
