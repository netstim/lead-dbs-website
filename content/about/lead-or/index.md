# Lead-OR

  

Lead-OR is a sister application to Lead-DBS and meant to assist research in the operation theatre when carrying our DBS surgeries. It directly builds upon Lead-DBS based coregistrations and segmentations and offers a real-time application to visualize invasive recordings (local field potentials and microelectrode recordings), as well as to model stimulation impact onto fiber tracts.
