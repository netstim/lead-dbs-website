Lead-DBS is coordinated today by the [Institute for Network Stimulation](https://www.netstim.org) at the
University Hospital Cologne, led by Andreas Horn; its people are listed on
[netstim.org](https://www.netstim.org/about-us/about-us/). Everyone who has contributed code is shown in the
[contributors graph on GitHub](https://github.com/netstim/leaddbs/graphs/contributors).
